# Third-party notices

## Quagga2

ThirtyStash uses **@ericblade/quagga2 1.12.1** for browser-side UPC/EAN barcode
scanning. The Docker build downloads the pinned browser bundle and serves it
locally at runtime.

Quagga2 project: https://github.com/ericblade/quagga2
